# Coverage gaps

Do not treat these areas as settled standards. Frame the decision, gather evidence, and ask for human acceptance before promoting a pattern to `rules.md`.

- First-run onboarding and permission education.
- Recovery UX after macOS microphone or screen/audio permission denial.
- Recording-in-progress duration, waveform, or confidence indicators.
- Confirmation or recovery behavior when stopping/finalizing a recording fails.
- Naming, renaming, deleting, and retention of recordings.
- Search, filtering, sorting, and transcript editing.
- Export progress, completion feedback, destination choice, and additional formats.
- Multiple remote speakers and unknown channel/speaker labels.
- Offline behavior and queued transcription.
- Dark mode and wider/resizable window behavior.
- Visual regression strategy and deterministic design lint rules.
- Accessibility acceptance targets beyond the current keyboard, labels, focus, contrast, and non-color requirements.

For a new gap, record the affected user/job, why current guidance is insufficient, evidence needed, and the decision owner if known.
